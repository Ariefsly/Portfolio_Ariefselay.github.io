Problem Background

Recording daily expenses is often a tedious task for many college students. Manual tracking feels overly complicated and time-consuming, leading to procrastination. As a result, expenses are poorly managed, culminating in financial struggles at the end of the month.

Research Findings

From our research and interviews with target users, we discovered several key insights:

  • Fast Flow: Users desire an instant way to record expenses without going through multiple steps (clicks).
  • Simple Visualization: Simple charts that quickly indicate financial status are highly preferred over complex numerical reports.
  • Jargon-Free: Advanced financial terminologies make users feel overwhelmed.

Design Solution

Based on these findings, I designed the "Saku App" interface focusing on a Minimalist Dashboard and a 3-Click Recording Flow. Users simply need to input the amount, select an intuitive category icon, and save.
